# Research Gap

Existing project management and workflow automation systems provide basic scheduling, task management, and resource tracking functionalities. However, most systems lack intelligent decision-making capabilities, predictive analytics, and AI-driven workflow optimization specifically tailored for production house environments.

Many existing solutions do not effectively predict project delays, optimize resource allocation, or provide real-time recommendations based on historical project data. Additionally, there is limited integration of Artificial Intelligence for automating production workflows and improving collaboration among stakeholders.

Therefore, there is a need for an AI-Powered Production House Management System that combines workflow automation, predictive analytics, intelligent scheduling, and resource optimization to improve productivity and operational efficiency.
